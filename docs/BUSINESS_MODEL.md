# Business Model (GDD §124)

## Free intro

- The vertical slice (Aric / Lantern Inn / Missing Travelers, ~30–60 min) is
  free forever. No account wall before the first return to Marla.
- Goal: let a player feel "the game remembered that" before paying anything.

## Subscription tiers (differentiated by usage, never power)

| Tier | Differentiators |
|---|---|
| Wanderer (free) | Intro campaign, 1 campaign slot, standard narration model |
| Chronicler (paid) | More monthly AI usage, 5 campaign slots, advanced models, image scenes |
| Worldbuilder (paid+) | Everything above + campaign creator, community publishing, voice narration |

## Hard rule: never pay-to-win

- Never sold: equipment, skill boosts, loot boxes, paid dice rerolls,
  clue purchases, or resurrection tokens.
- Money buys canvas and paint (slots, usage, models, art, creator tools) —
  never outcomes inside the world. A paying player's sword swing uses the
  same engine check as a free player's.
