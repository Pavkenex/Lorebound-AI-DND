# Positioning & Product Identity (GDD §126–128)

## Internal positioning statement

> A persistent role-playing world where you can attempt anything and the
> world remembers what happened.

Never: "chat with an AI dungeon master." The product is a world that
remembers, not a chatbot with a fantasy prompt.

## Guardrails

- Marketing copy never leads with the technology. Lead with the world, the
  choice, and the memory. AI is how, never what.
- Screenshots show the world (leads, pack, consequences), not model names.
- Every claim must be reproducible in the vertical slice: attempting anything,
  NPC memory, multi-solution mystery, permanent consequences.
- Name the feeling, not the stack: rain, debt, lantern-light — not models.
