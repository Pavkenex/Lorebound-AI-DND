# Campaign Creator & Player-Authored Worlds (GDD §122–123)

## Creator defines (constraints in, canon out)

The creator sets the canon frame; the AI only fills minor detail inside it:

1. World premise (one paragraph: what is this place, what is wrong with it)
2. Regions (named areas with one-line identities)
3. Factions (goals, methods, headquarters — as in Ravenford's three)
4. Major NPCs (role, want, secret — minor NPCs are generated)
5. Themes (e.g. debt, rain, memory) used as narration guardrails
6. Hidden truths (the real answers behind the mysteries; never shown to players)
7. Starting situation (opening scene constraints: arrival, weather, hook)
8. Allowed races / classes / magic (closed list; the glossary rule applies)
9. Campaign tone (grim, warm, wry) with a narration length budget

Rule: anything the creator leaves blank is filled by the AI *as minor
detail only* — new major NPCs, truths, or threads require creator approval.

## Long-term items (post-MVP backlog, not slice scope)

- Custom narrator personalities (voice + diction presets within tone bounds)
- Community campaigns (publish, rate, fork; provenance-checked against IP glossary)
- Campaign replay summaries ("while you were away" / season recaps)
- Generative scene art (paid tiers; never pay-to-win)
- Voice narration (paid tiers; concise-mode aware)
- Housing, politics, trade (persistent ownership + faction-economy loops)
- Mod support (versioned content packs against the content schema)
- Campaign export (portable JSON: canon + truths + memory threads)
